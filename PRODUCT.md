# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated within the brief's stated preference: React + Vite + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4 + lucide-react, prerendered to static HTML per page at build time so every page is indexable without client rendering. Deploy target: Vercel (static output). No backend; quote submissions go to a form endpoint configured by environment variable.

## Users

Primarily businesses — traders, shops and manufacturers in and around Panvel / Navi Mumbai — who need goods moved by road and want one local company to move it. Individuals may also enquire, but copy speaks to business senders first. (Confirmed by owner-side answer, 2026-10-28.)

## Product Purpose

The marketing website for M J Cargo Movers, a goods transport company in New Panvel, Navi Mumbai, that runs its own trucks. Its job is to make the business findable for local searches and turn a visitor with goods to move into a quote request (form, and phone/WhatsApp once a number is published). Success = qualified quote requests with pickup, delivery and cargo details filled in.

## Positioning

A local road freight company in New Panvel with its own trucks — not an agent (confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-29): a real office (Shree Ganesh Plaza 1, behind Netrajyot Hospital, Khanda Colony) close to where the Mumbai–Pune Expressway begins, that moves road freight for its customers.

## Operating Context

- Customers describe a consignment by pickup point, delivery point, type of goods and approximate weight/quantity, then receive a quotation.
- Coordination happens by phone/in person; there is **no tracking system** and the site must not imply one.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: M J Cargo Movers. Logo supplied by the owner (2026-10-28): a navy box truck with a white serif "MJ", "CARGO MOVERS" in green, a green truck outline, and the tagline "Transporting cargo with care". Source file: `assets-src/brand/logo.png`; site variants are generated by `npm run brand`.

## Product Principles

1. Never claim what isn't verified — no invented numbers, reviews, routes, guarantees or tracking.
2. Every page leads to a quote request with the details we need to price a load.
3. Local and concrete beats generic: the real office, the real neighbourhood, the real roads.
4. Contact options appear only when they work (no dead call buttons).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard operability, visible focus, AA contrast, reduced-motion support, labelled form fields with inline errors. Many visitors will be on mid-range Android phones on mobile data — keep pages light.
