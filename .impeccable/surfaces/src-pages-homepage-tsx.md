# Surface brief: marketing site (home, services, about, contact)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Panvel / Navi Mumbai businesses with goods to move by road. Action: start a quote (pickup → delivery) in its working form. Proof: real office address, real regional photography; no invented reviews, numbers or routes. Constraints: navy/white pinned by brief, accent is the logo green (owner's logo, 2026-10-28, replaced amber); phone hidden until provided; no tracking implied.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the road the goods take out of Panvel. It refuses the category default of truck-hero + icon service cards + stats band.

OWN-WORLD: Night-asphalt navy grounds stepping to daylight white; lane paint in the logo green (#579e06) reserved for the primary action, the route line, the runway light trails, and the hero truck's headboard lights and lane; road-sign panels (filled navy, 2px lamp inset border, 1.75rem corners) wherever the page asks a question; sections overlap as sheets on rounded shoulders, never hard horizontal cuts; a floating rounded header with honest offset shadow; Archivo at full width (125%) as the gantry voice; pill controls that invert navy↔white on press. Long-exposure light trails (white and logo green) run only in runway strips on open night at the foot of night headers, never behind copy. Motion: the process lane paints itself in on scroll; no other scroll effects.

STORY: Visitor learns this is a New Panvel company offering full truck load transport in its own trucks (a whole truck per load, never part loads), warehousing and freight forwarding, believes it is local and straight-dealing (real office, quote before booking), and starts a quote.
